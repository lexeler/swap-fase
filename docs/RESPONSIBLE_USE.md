# Responsible Use Policy

swap-fase replaces a face in a live webcam stream with the face from a photo you
provide. This is powerful technology that can be misused to deceive, impersonate,
or harm people. **By using swap-fase you agree to the terms below.** If you cannot
or will not follow them, do not use this software.

This software is intended for **personal, local, and experimental use only**.

## You must

- **Get consent.** Obtain clear, informed permission from any person whose face
  you use as a target photo *and* from anyone whose face is being swapped on
  camera. Do not use a person's likeness without their knowledge and agreement.
- **Be transparent.** If you show swapped output to other people (for example on a
  video call), make it clear that the face is altered. Never present a face swap
  as a genuine, unaltered image of a real person.
- **Comply with the law.** Follow all applicable laws and regulations in your
  jurisdiction, including those covering privacy, likeness/publicity rights,
  defamation, harassment, fraud, and synthetic-media ("deepfake") disclosure.
- **Follow platform rules.** Respect the terms of service and acceptable-use
  policies of any video-call, streaming, or social platform you use the virtual
  camera with. Many platforms restrict or prohibit synthetic faces and
  impersonation.
- **Respect the model licenses.** The InsightFace `buffalo_l` and
  `inswapper_128.onnx` models are licensed for **non-commercial / research use
  only**. Keep your use within those terms.

## You must not

- **Impersonate** any real person — public figure or private individual — in a way
  intended or likely to deceive.
- **Commit fraud or deception**: identity verification / KYC bypass, financial
  scams, fake "proof of life", catfishing, or any attempt to make someone believe
  they are seeing or talking to a different real person.
- **Harass, intimidate, defame, or degrade** anyone, including creating
  non-consensual, sexual, abusive, or humiliating content.
- **Target minors** in any way, or create sexual or exploitative content involving
  anyone.
- **Spread disinformation** or fabricate "evidence" of someone saying or doing
  something they did not.
- **Use anyone's face without consent**, or in any context they have not agreed to.
- **Use it commercially** or in any published product (the models forbid this).
